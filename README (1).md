## Business problem

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market. For two weeks she has written her daily sales in a notebook and kept a rough record of stock, but she has no clear picture of how the business is performing. She wants to know whether sales are growing, which days matter most, which products she must restock, and where she should focus her effort. As her freelance analyst, I answered these questions with core Python only (lists, dictionaries, functions and f-strings, with no pandas or NumPy) and delivered a printed Stall Performance Report with recommendations.

## Top three findings

1. **Sales are growing:** week 2 sales were KES 24,400 against KES 22,050 in week 1, a **10.7%** week-on-week increase.
2. **Weekends drive the business:** Saturday and Sunday average **KES 4,900** a day versus **KES 2,685** on weekdays, which is **82.5%** higher.
3. **Two products need restocking now:** Tomatoes (18 kg vs a 25 kg reorder level) and Sukuma (9 kg vs 15 kg). Avocado has the best margin at **42.9%**.
